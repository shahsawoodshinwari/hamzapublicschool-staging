Admissions

Join the Hamza Public School family. We welcome students from all backgrounds who are eager to learn and grow.

Admission Process

Online Inquiry

Fill out the initial inquiry form to express your interest.

Campus Visit

Schedule a tour of our Landi Kotal campus to see our facilities.

Assessment

Students undergo a basic evaluation of their academic level.

Enrollment

Submit required documents and complete the fee process.

Required Documents

  • Original Birth Certificate / B-Form
  • Previous School Leaving Certificate
  • 4 Passport size photographs
  • Copy of Parent/Guardian CNIC
  • Previous Academic Records (if applicable)
